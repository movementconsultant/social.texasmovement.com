What Gather Is

Gather is a new way to mark the moments that change your life. Not just a planning tool — a full experience layer that wraps around your ceremony before, during, and long after the day itself. Gather is powered by Circle OS™, our ceremony operating system that keeps the planning calm and the memories sharp. Circle OS runs quietly in the background so your experience stays fully human in the foreground.

Signature Gather Moments

Gather isn't just logistics — it's built around named moments that create emotional anchors in your ceremony. These moments are exclusive to the Gather platform.

Pause™

A short, intentional moment of silence held just before the ceremony begins. No music, no movement — just presence. It signals to everyone in the room that something real is about to happen.

Echo™

A post-ceremony recap that turns guest photos, shared messages, and recorded moments into a short film or narrative keepsake. Delivered within days of the event as a living memory you can share or keep private.

Blessing Circle

A structured moment where guests share a wish, hope, or memory with the couple or family — without the pressure of public speaking. Warm, comfortable, and always meaningful.

Thread™

A living thread of guest messages collected before and after the ceremony. It becomes a digital heirloom that grows over time — first-year anniversary notes, milestone check-ins, and more.

Question Stone

Guests receive a single, beautifully framed question to reflect on during the event. Their responses become part of the Echo™ recap and ceremony keepsake. Thoughtful participation without awkward ice-breakers.

Letter Loop

A structured exchange where the couple or family writes personal letters — to each other, to their child, to their future selves — sealed and delivered at a future milestone. A promise made tangible.

Questions We Hear Often

Is this just another planning app?
Gather is not a task manager or a vendor directory. It's a full ceremony experience layer — built around emotional design, named rituals, and memory capture — that wraps around your milestone from planning through the aftermath. Think of it less like software and more like a thoughtful co-designer.
Do I have to be very online or on every social platform?
Not at all. Gather is designed to be as private or as shareable as you choose. You control what gets captured, what gets shared, and who sees it. Some families use Gather for a completely private ceremony with zero social output. Others use Gather: Connected™ to create beautiful, intentional content. Both are fully supported.
Can I use Gather if I already have a planner or venue?
Yes — Gather is designed to work alongside your existing planner and venue, not replace them. Circle OS™ integrates into whatever structure is already in place. If you're a planner, ask about Circle OS for Pros™ for a deeper integration layer.
What about privacy and guest data?
We take this seriously. Guest information is used only to support your ceremony experience and is never sold, shared, or used for marketing. Your Echo™ recap and Thread™ archive belong to you. We don't use your ceremony content for any purpose beyond delivering your experience.
Where is Gather available right now?
Gather is currently in private beta, with capacity limited to a small number of ceremonies during our launch period. We're based in Texas and serving families across the US. Join the waitlist to be considered for early access — we'll reach out when a spot opens that matches your timeline.
How much does this cost, roughly?
Gather experiences are priced based on the level of support, the size of your ceremony, and which product line fits your milestone. Pricing starts at an accessible tier for smaller, more intimate ceremonies and scales from there. We'll share full pricing during your intake conversation after you join the waitlist.
Can my family handle this if they're not tech-savvy?
Absolutely. Gather is designed with non-technical guests in mind. Guest participation happens through simple, familiar formats — no apps to download, no accounts to create. If a guest can send a text or click a link, they can participate fully in every Gather moment.
Is this only for weddings?
No — while Gather: Union™ is built specifically for weddings, the broader Gather line serves engagements, births, naming ceremonies, and any milestone that deserves more than a party. If you're marking something that changed your life, Gather was built for it.
