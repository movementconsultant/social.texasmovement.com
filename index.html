Our Standards

Safety Without Prejudice

No discrimination based on race, gender, sexuality, or how you present. If you move with respect, you're welcome in the room. Promoters and staff are briefed on inclusion, de-escalation, and get-home-safe norms.

Inclusive, High-Energy Rooms

Bottle culture, dance floor, and queer-friendly corners coexist when the night is designed for it. Queer, trans, and non-binary guests aren't tolerated—they're expected and protected.

Data Over Vibes

We measure heads, spend, and repeat attendance so nights can be tuned like a performance program, not left to chance.

We Remember

We remember packed rooms, real DJs, and uncles and aunties watching the door. TMS brings that care into 2026—no funny business, no weak nights. Built on west side soul and south side discipline.
