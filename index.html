Where Texas Movement shows up in public.

The social layer—follow the work across platforms, see live gatherings through Gather, and engage on clear terms.

Texas Movement Social is the public layer of the ecosystem. It is for the public, the community, and event attendees. It solves a simple problem: knowing where the system shows up and how to engage with it. It works by organizing the real accounts by lane, running events through Gather, and distributing updates deliberately. It connects to Media, which produces much of the signal, and to the founder profile at AlexanderMathai.com. This is not a site about social-media strategy. It is the disciplined public face of Texas Movement—where to follow, where to gather, and how to engage.

Gather events

What Gather is

Gather is the events and gatherings module inside Texas Movement Social—structured live presence, online and offline, with clear scope.

Types of gatherings

Sessions, workshops, and events tied to the lanes—training, product, systems, and recovery—run with clear expectations and outcomes.

How to stay informed

Gather schedules and announcements are shared deliberately through the main lanes and, where relevant, through direct event communication.

What to expect

Calm, structured experiences. No chaos, no hype—just clear programming aligned with the lane hosting the gathering.

Community norms

Calm, on-topic interaction. Engagement is welcomed when it is grounded, relevant, and in good faith.

Signal over noise. The public presence favors clarity and substance over volume or spectacle.

Respect the lanes. Keep questions and conversation aligned with the lane you are in; that makes better work possible.

Boundaries are upheld. Legal, medical, and other boundaries stated across the ecosystem apply here too.

Ecosystem connections

Media produces much of the signal; Social is where that signal reaches the public and where Gather lives.

Every lane links back up to the hub at TexasMovement.com and sideways into Social, so public presence stays coherent.

The founder layer at AlexanderMathai.com stays the personal authority anchor behind all public activity.

Frequently asked questions

What is Texas Movement Social?

It is the public layer where Texas Movement shows up across platforms, plus events through Gather. It is not a social-media-tips site.

Where should I follow?

Follow by lane—HERO for product, Performance for training, Media for documentation, and the founder for perspective. Links are organized on this page.

What is Gather?

Gather is the events and gatherings module—structured live presence, online and offline, with clear scope and expectations.

What’s the content cadence?

Deliberate, not constant. Signal over volume, consistent with how Texas Movement Media operates.

What kind of engagement is welcomed?

Calm, on-topic, good-faith interaction. This is a disciplined public space, not a hype feed.

How does Social connect to the rest of Texas Movement?

Media produces the signal; Social is where it reaches the public and where gatherings happen, all linked back to the hub and founder layers.
